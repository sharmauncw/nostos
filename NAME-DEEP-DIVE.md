# Name Deep Dive — Roommate-Matching Company (working name: AmigoNest)

Date: Nov 8, 2026 · Status: WORKING NAME ONLY — nothing public until formal clearance.

## The finding (one line)
The global consumer namespace is saturated: every name built from category
vocabulary (room, nest, flat, mate, buddy, pal, key, land, vibe, settle) is
already used, most of them by direct competitors. Survivors are coined
compounds or repurposed words from outside the category (the Zillow / Bumble /
Airbnb pattern).

## The graveyard (screened Nov 8, 2026, web evidence)
- **Roomigo / RooMigo** — THREE users: Irish compatibility-flatmate company
  (2016, Dublin, roomigo.io, now closed), an ACTIVE room-rental app
  (Roomigo, Beauvia&CO LLC, app stores, policy updated Oct 2026), and
  RoomiGro (Egypt roommate app). DEAD.
- **Roomgo** — established global roommate-listings platform. DEAD.
- **Roomi, Roomster, Roomies, Roomy, Roomzie, iROOMit** — all live roommate
  brands. The Room- prefix also fights Roomi's search gravity forever. DEAD.
- **Nest-anything** — NextNest (India, rentals + roommate finder, live,
  tagline nearly identical to ours), NestMate (UK, nestmate.llc — compatibility
  housemate matching with "NeighbourScore™", launching 2026 — our exact
  product), NearNest (India student housing), Nestigo (real-estate SaaS +
  Indian home-services app), Nestora / Nestoria (property platforms on three
  continents), Nestly, Nestaway. The most crowded namespace in the category.
- **BuddyUp** — "Buddy Up!" is literally a London flatmate app; BuddyUpLiving
  is UK student housing. DEAD.
- **VibeCheck** — 4+ products (party game, dating AI, couples app). A phrase,
  not an ownable name. DEAD. (Vibe also dates fast.)
- **Pally** — YC-backed AI company owns pally.com. DEAD.
- **SpareKey** — live Indian house-sharing app (sparekeyapp.com). DEAD.
  KeyNest also occupies the key stem.
- **Landly / Landing / Landed** — Landly (India property software), Landing
  (US furnished-apartment company, 20k units), Landed (homebuying fintech).
  DEAD/muddy.
- **MateMatch / CasaMate** — "mate" reads as partner/mating in the US; wrong
  for a safety-first brand. DEAD on language alone.
- **FlatMatch** — "flat" is India/UK only (her catch). DEAD on language.
- **Common** — Common Living, a major US coliving company. DEAD.
- **Kindred** — funded home-swap company. DEAD. **Alcove** — NC roommate
  platform. DEAD. **Colive** — Bangalore coliving company. DEAD.
- **Settly / Settler** — Settly is an active relocation company; "settler"
  carries colonial baggage in the US/CA/AU. DEAD.
- **Yaar / Yaari / Dosti / Sangam / Mitra / Saathi / Adda** — Yaari is a live
  Indian social app; Dosti is a Mumbai realty brand; Adda collides with
  Adda247; the rest are crowded Indian brands. Desi-only names also cap the
  global story.
- **Pally / Homely** — "homely" means unattractive in US English. A reminder
  that every candidate needs a per-market language check.

## Rules the evidence teaches
1. If a name sounds obvious for this category, it is taken.
2. Avoid the Room-, Nest-, Land- prefixes entirely — they are SEO wars
   against funded incumbents, not just trademark risks.
3. Coined compounds (two warm roots fused) are the ownable pattern.
4. The name must pass the radio test in every market: hear it once, spell it,
   no wrong meaning in US English, Hindi, or Spanish.
5. NO public use before formal clearance: USPTO + EUIPO + India IPO,
   .com domain, and social handles — the AI Radar rename is the standing
   lesson. Internal build proceeds under the working name regardless.

## Finalists (first screens passed; formal clearance still owed)
1. **AmigoNest** — WORKING NAME. Zero direct users found. Amigo = friend in
   Spanish, slang in the US, pop-culture-known in India; nest = home. Coined
   compound = ownable. Known tax: the "nest" half sits in a crowded
   neighborhood (NextNest/NestMate), which costs some search clarity even
   though the compound itself is distinct. Tagline: "Find a roommate who
   lives like you."
2. **Dost** — dark horse, not yet formally screened. Means "friend" across
   Hindi/Urdu/Persian/Turkish; reads as a clean coined word in the US;
   teachable in one line ("Dost means friend"). Watch: YourDost adjacency in
   Indian wellness (different category).
3. **RoomVibe** — no blocker found, but descriptive = weak trademark posture,
   and "vibe" ages. Fallback only.

## Round 4 — the gravity brief (Nov 8; her words: AmigoNest "feels small,
not like a big company")
Diagnosis: compounds with cute suffixes read small. Big-feeling names are
short, single-word, confident (Sonder, Roam, Common — all taken).
- **AptAmigo** — NEW FACT: "Amigo" in US housing is already occupied by
  AptAmigo (Chicago apartment platform, founded 2015, ~155 employees,
  "the match.com of apartment hunting") + Amigo Living (UK student
  housing). Weakens the whole amigo family in her home market.
- **Aroha** — Aroha Properties (UK agents), Aroha Developers (India
  luxury real estate), Aroha United. OUT.
- **Nostos** — SURVIVES. Greek for "homecoming" (Odyssey; the root of
  "nostalgia" = nostos + algos, homecoming + pain). Only small uses
  found (a boutique "Nostos Stays", an AR studio) — no platform, no
  housing company, no major holder. One word, six letters, epic story,
  company gravity. Cost: unfamiliar word — needs one line of teaching
  (the Sonder playbook). Recommended Nov 8 as the gravity pick;
  AmigoNest remains the warm fallback.

## Gate before anything public
Formal clearance on the finalist: trademark registers (US, EU, India),
.com availability, Instagram/TikTok/X handles, app-store name check.
If AmigoNest fails, Dost is next; the company does not launch on a name
that has not cleared.

## DECISION (Nov 8, 2026)
She delegated the call ("U decide"). **Chosen: Nostos** — tagline
"Find your people. Come home." It answers her gravity brief (short,
single-word, company-weight), screened clean of any major holder, and
the homecoming story is the product's emotional core. AmigoNest was
the runner-up (weakened by AptAmigo's US housing presence). Repo:
github.com/sharmauncw/nostos · Live: sharmauncw.github.io/nostos.
Formal clearance still gates public launch.
